What this role is

CPC Direct is a digital business-development company. We build ventures and deliver digital services — automation, product build and advisory — including work with Access Emerging Markets. The GM owns the outcome: learn the apps and services deeply, find the growth, and lead the effort day to day.

  • Take the business from startup to profitable in one year — you set the plan and drive it.
  • Learn every app and service well enough to sell and explain them with confidence.
  • Hire and lead a small team to support the effort.
  • Be the face of the company — clear, articulate, and credible with clients and partners.
The package: salary + commission + profit-sharing. This is a thinking job, not a routine one — real ownership, real upside if you deliver.

Terms of this application processCPC Direct Ventures Ltd · Abuja, Nigeria · September 2026

We will never ask you for money. There is no application fee, no training fee, no deposit — at any stage, ever. If anyone claiming to represent CPC Direct asks you to pay for anything, it is not us. Please report it to careers@cpc-direct.com.

1. What this is

This page is the first stage of hiring for the General Manager role at CPC Direct. Taking part is free and entirely voluntary, and you can stop at any point before you submit.

2. What we collect

  • Required: your name, gender, and one contact detail (email or phone).
  • Optional: your age, location, and any links you choose to share.
  • Your answers to the written questions, including the conversational ones — all of which you may skip.
  • A voice recording you make in your browser. It is audio only; no video or image of you is captured, and your camera is never accessed.

3. How your application is reviewed

The first step is reviewed automatically. An automated system reads your written answers and scores them to help us shortlist fairly and consistently. Based on that review we decide on next steps, which may also be automated or handled by a member of our team.

Your voice recording is not scored automatically — it is listened to by our hiring team. No decision to reject you is made by automated means alone at the stages that follow: a person reviews anyone we consider taking forward.

4. Who sees your information

Only people involved in hiring at CPC Direct. We do not sell your information, share it for marketing, publish it, or pass it to anyone outside the company for their own purposes. We use ordinary service providers to operate this process — website hosting, email delivery, and the automated review — and they process your information only on our instructions.

5. How long we keep it

Up to 12 months from the day you submit, so we can consider you for this and any similar role that opens in that time. After that it is deleted. You can ask us to delete it sooner at any point, and we will.

6. Your choices and your rights

  • Every question marked optional can be left blank, and it will not count against you.
  • You may withdraw your application, or your consent, at any time.
  • You may ask for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it.
  • To do any of these, email careers@cpc-direct.com.

We handle your information in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

7. Honesty

Your answers and your recording should be your own work and truthful. If we find that material information was false or that someone else completed this for you, we will end consideration of your application.

8. No offer of employment

Submitting this form does not create a contract, an offer, or a guarantee of an interview or a role. We are not able to give individual feedback on every application, and we will only contact you if we are taking things further.

9. Questions

Anything at all about this process or your information — write to careers@cpc-direct.com and a person will answer you.
